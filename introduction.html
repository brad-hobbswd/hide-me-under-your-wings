There are battles people carry that never appear on the surface. A person can walk into a room, greet everyone with a smile, fulfill responsibilities, care for a family, serve in ministry, and continue showing up for others while quietly struggling to hold everything together. The people around them may see strength and dependability, but they may never see the exhaustion, disappointment, grief, or fear that waits behind that familiar smile. Some wounds are easy to recognize. Others are carried silently because the person does not know how to explain what hurts, or because they have become so accustomed to being strong for everyone else that they no longer know how to ask for help.

There are seasons when the weight becomes difficult to describe. One disappointment follows another. Responsibilities do not pause simply because the heart is tired. Questions remain unanswered, relationships become strained, and the encouragement a person has freely given to others can feel difficult to find when they need it themselves. In those moments, even ordinary days can feel heavy. A person may wonder how much longer they can keep going, whether anyone truly understands, or where they can turn when their own strength seems to be running out.

This book begins in that honest place. It does not pretend that faith makes every painful circumstance disappear, nor does it suggest that a believer will never become weary, discouraged, or overwhelmed. Faith does not require us to deny what hurts. It invites us to bring the truth of our lives before the One who knows us completely. Jesus sees the struggles that others overlook. He understands the words we cannot find, the prayers that come out as tears, and the burdens we have carried for far too long.

Ashley's journey gives this message a human face. Her experience reflects the struggle of a heart that has tried to remain strong while facing more than it knows how to carry. Through her story, we are invited to consider what happens when pressure, disappointment, and emotional exhaustion begin to wear down the strength a person has relied upon. Her journey is not presented as a claim that every person's experience is the same. Rather, it offers a point of connection for readers who recognize something of their own hidden battles in the struggle to keep moving forward.

At the center of this journey is the promise found in Psalm 91:4: "He shall cover you with His feathers, and under His wings shall you trust." The picture is tender and powerful. It speaks of nearness, shelter, and the security of belonging to the One who watches over us. To find refuge beneath the wings of the Lord is to turn toward Him with our whole selves, not only with the parts of our lives that appear strong and put together. It is to bring Him our fear as well as our faith, our questions as well as our confidence, and our weariness as well as our worship.

God's covering should never be mistaken for a promise that storms will never come. Scripture does not teach us that a life of faith is free from sorrow, conflict, loss, or uncertainty. Instead, it continually points us toward the faithfulness of God in the middle of life's changing circumstances. His presence remains when answers are delayed. His love remains when people disappoint us. His strength is available when our own strength has reached its limit. We may still have to walk through difficult days, but we do not have to walk through them believing that we have been abandoned.

As you read these pages, you may recognize a season of your own. Perhaps you have spent years caring for others while neglecting the wounds within your own heart. Perhaps someone you trusted disappointed you, or circumstances left you feeling unseen and misunderstood. Maybe you are carrying grief that has no easy explanation, or you are simply tired in a way that rest alone does not seem to resolve. Whatever has brought you to this book, you do not have to hide your pain from Jesus. You can come honestly, without pretending, and allow His Word to remind you that your life is still held in His care.

This is an invitation to slow down and make room for the presence of God. As you encounter Scripture, reflection, and Ashley's journey, take time to consider what the Lord may be speaking to your heart. Let the words lead you toward prayer, renewed trust, and a deeper understanding of the refuge available in Jesus. Healing is not always immediate, and restoration may unfold one step at a time. There is no need to measure your progress against someone else's journey. Bring each day to the Lord and allow Him to meet you there.

My prayer is that these pages will encourage the weary, comfort the hurting, and remind every reader that being overwhelmed does not mean being forgotten. May you discover that you can lay down the pressure to appear unbreakable and find rest in the presence of the One who never grows weary. May your faith be strengthened, your hope renewed, and your heart reminded that the Lord remains faithful through every season.

Welcome to Hide Me Under Your Wings Lord. Come with the burdens you have carried, the questions you have not been able to answer, and the hope you may be afraid to speak aloud. There is room to seek Him here. There is room to breathe, to pray, and to begin again. May you find refuge beneath His wings and learn to trust the One who holds you close.
