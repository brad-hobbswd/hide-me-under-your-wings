Chapter 10: Stepping Out of the Wings as the Restored One

HomeTable of ContentsBegin Reading

By the time Ashley reached this part of her journey, she could look back on seasons that once seemed impossible to survive. She had known betrayal, exhaustion, loneliness, uncertainty, and the quiet struggle of trying to remain strong when her heart was tired. Those experiences had left their mark, but they no longer defined every part of her life. She had begun to understand that healing did not mean becoming untouched by pain. It meant learning to live with renewed trust in Jesus and greater wisdom about how to care for the life He had given her.

The woman standing at this point in the story was not someone who had discovered how to avoid every storm. She was someone who had learned where to turn when storms came. Her strength was no longer measured by how much she could endure without speaking. She was learning to be honest, to seek help when necessary, to establish healthy boundaries, and to receive rest without shame. Her heart felt lighter because she no longer believed she had to carry everything alone.

"It is God who arms me with strength and makes my way perfect."

Psalm 18:32

When Ashley considered the hidden seasons, she could see that they had taught her lessons she wanted to carry forward. She had learned to bring her pain to God, to examine hurtful words without accepting condemnation, to distinguish forgiveness from pretending harm had never happened, and to let Scripture shape the way she understood herself. She had also learned that seeking refuge in the Lord did not mean withdrawing from every responsibility or relationship. It meant allowing His presence and truth to guide the way she lived.

"To give them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning."

Isaiah 61:3

There were still memories she would not have chosen and circumstances she could not rewrite. Yet she no longer viewed her future only through the lens of what had hurt her. Hope was returning, and with it came the willingness to make plans, rebuild relationships where it was wise, and take steps she had once been too overwhelmed to consider. She could acknowledge the ashes without believing they were the end of her story.

One truth had become especially important to Ashley: walking forward did not mean leaving God's protection behind. The image of being sheltered beneath His wings was not a place she had to abandon once she felt stronger. It described the trust she wanted to carry into every season. Whether life brought peace or uncertainty, she could continue seeking Jesus, depending on His Word, and allowing His presence to shape her choices.

"He brought me up out of a horrible pit and set my feet upon a rock."

Psalm 40:2

Ashley's story became a testimony not because every question had an easy answer, but because she had learned that the Lord remained faithful through the questions. She had been strengthened, corrected, comforted, and reminded that her life was held by One whose love did not change with circumstances. The journey beneath His wings had not made her invulnerable. It had taught her where to find refuge, how to receive grace, and how to keep walking with hope.

And that is the invitation this book leaves with every reader. You may be at the beginning of a difficult season, somewhere in the middle of a long struggle, or just beginning to recognize how much healing has taken place. Wherever you find yourself, you can turn toward Jesus today. You do not have to wait until you feel strong enough, have every answer, or know exactly what comes next. Come honestly, trust Him with the next step, and continue walking beneath His wings.

To the reader:

Your journey may be longer or different from Ashley's, and your next step may not look like anyone else's. Do not measure your healing by another person's timeline. Keep seeking Jesus, receive wise support, and let His Word guide your decisions. The storm does not have to define you, and the pain you have carried does not have to write the final chapter of your life. There is still room for hope, restoration, and renewed strength beneath His wings.
