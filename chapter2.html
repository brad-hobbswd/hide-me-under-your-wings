Chapter 2: The Cave Season Nobody Knew About

HomeTable of Contents

After the night Ashley sat in her car and finally admitted how deeply she was struggling, she entered a season she would later describe as her cave season. The name brought to mind David, who found himself in a cave while facing danger and uncertainty. Ashley's circumstances were different, but she understood something of the loneliness of a hidden place. Her cave was not a physical shelter. It was the quiet space she began seeking when the demands of life felt greater than the strength she had left.

She started declining invitations and allowing some calls to go unanswered. Plans that once seemed easy became difficult to face, and people who did not know what she was carrying sometimes interpreted her quietness as distance. Ashley was not trying to punish anyone or disappear from the lives of those she loved. She was exhausted and needed room to understand what had happened inside her. Even so, withdrawing from everyone was not the same as finding healing, and she would have to learn the difference between isolation and seeking refuge in God.

Some of her quietest moments came during long drives without a destination, late evenings sitting on the floor of her bedroom, and the stillness that followed when the day was finally over. At times she opened her Bible without knowing exactly what to read. She did not always have a complete prayer ready. Sometimes she simply whispered the name of Jesus and allowed the tears to come. In those moments, Psalm 17:8 became especially meaningful to her.

"Keep me as the apple of Your eye; hide me under the shadow of Your wings."

Psalm 17:8

The words gave language to a need she had struggled to explain. Ashley began to understand that seeking God's presence was not an attempt to pretend her problems did not exist. It was a decision to bring those problems to the One who could meet her in the middle of them. Refuge did not mean that she would never have to return to difficult conversations, responsibilities, or decisions. It meant that she did not have to face them as though she had been left alone.

As the quiet continued, emotions she had pushed aside began to surface. Anger, regret, grief, and shame all demanded attention. At first, their return frightened her. She had hoped that getting away from the noise would make the pain disappear, but silence often reveals what busyness has kept buried. Ashley had to be honest about what hurt before she could begin to understand what healing might require.

"The Lord is near to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit."

Psalm 34:18

This promise reminded Ashley that brokenness did not place her beyond the reach of God. She did not have to hide her emotions from Him or wait until she felt stronger to pray. Her healing would not happen all at once, and there would be days when she felt progress and days when old feelings returned. Still, she began to see that weakness was not proof of failure. It was an honest place from which she could seek the grace and strength of the Lord.

"My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness."

2 Corinthians 12:9

Ashley's cave season was becoming a place of honesty, but it could not become a permanent hiding place from life. The quiet was helping her listen, pray, and recognize what needed attention. As she began to find steadier ground, another part of her healing would require courage: facing the pain caused by words spoken by people she had trusted. The refuge she was finding in Jesus would have to hold her not only through loneliness, but also through the difficult work of responding to hurt without allowing bitterness to take root.

To the reader walking through a hidden season:

You may need quiet, rest, and time with God, especially when life has become overwhelming. Let that time draw you toward the Lord rather than cut you off from every source of healthy support. Seek wise and trustworthy people when you need help, and remember that asking for support does not mean your faith is weak. God can meet you in the quiet, guide you through difficult decisions, and give you strength for the next step.
